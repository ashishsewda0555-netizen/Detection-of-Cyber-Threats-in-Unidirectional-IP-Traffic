OPTION B — nping Spoofed Flood (Windows Nmap on Attacker PC):
  NOTE: nping does NOT support --rand-source natively on Windows.
  Use this workaround to simulate distributed source IPs:
  FOR /L %i IN (1,1,20) DO nping --tcp -p 80 --flags syn --rate 500 -c 5000 --source-ip 10.0.%i.1 192.168.100.2
  This cycles 20 different source IPs to create src_ip_entropy > 0.
